Unlike prompt-only LLM skills that approximate multi-period schedules in token space, BI-13 executes exact pure-Python and NumPy statutory rules in volatile container RAM:

  • (2) 42 U.S.C. Sec. 1396a(bb) Medicaid PPS MEI Indexing & Change-in-Scope Rebasing [42 U.S.C. Sec. 1396a(bb)(2)-(3) Medicaid FQHC Prospective Payment System] — PPS_Active = Cost_Per_Visit if |(Cost_Per_Visit - PPS_0*(1+MEI))/PPS_0*(1+MEI)| >= 5% else PPS_0*(1+MEI)
  • (3) 42 U.S.C. Sec. 256b(a)(5)(A)-(B) 340B Duplicate-Discount & Diversion Audit [42 U.S.C. Sec. 256b(a)(5)(A)-(B) Public Health Service Act Section 340B] — Escrow_340B = (Claims_DupDisc * Avg_Mfg_Rebate) + (Claims_Diversion * Avg_340B_Spread)
  • (4) 42 U.S.C. Sec. 1396a(bb)(5) Medicaid MCO Supplemental Wraparound Settlement [42 U.S.C. Sec. 1396a(bb)(5) Quarterly State Supplemental FQHC Wraparound Rule] — Wraparound_Due = max(0, Visits_MCO * PPS_Rate - MCO_Interim_Paid - TPL_Copay)
