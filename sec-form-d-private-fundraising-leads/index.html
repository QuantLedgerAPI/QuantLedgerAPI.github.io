1. Four Statutory & Tax Intelligence Layers Computed Per Form D Issuer

  • IRC § 409A Material Financing Safe-Harbor Reset (26 CFR § 1.409A-1(b)(5)(iv)(B)): Identifies operating C-Corps closing ≥ $250,000 of equity or convertible/option securities that require an updated 409A common-stock valuation (see BI-48 409A Calculator).
  • Dual-State Foreign Qualification & 57-Jurisdiction Tax Rate: Compares state of incorporation (e.g. Delaware) against principal operating state (e.g. California, New York, Texas) and computes combined federal + state statutory corporate tax rate.
  • IRC § 1202 Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS) Screen: Evaluates C-Corp status, domestic incorporation, gross offering cap (≤ $50M), and non-excluded trade categories under 26 U.S.C. § 1202(e)(3).
  • 17 CFR § 230.503 15-Day Timeliness & SEC § 15(a) Finder-Fee Screen: Measures exact calendar days from dateOfFirstSale to filingDate and flags disclosed finder fees lacking a registered broker-dealer CRD.
